# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences, in priority order:

1. **Prospective travelers** — couples, families, and small groups (Singapore, Europe, UAE per testimonials) planning a premium multi-island Indonesia trip. They browse on both desktop and mobile, compare against polished competitors (Jacada, Black Tomato, Audley), and judge trustworthiness from visual quality before reading a word.
2. **Upwork clients / recruiters** — the site doubles as a portfolio piece proving production-grade frontend work. Pixel-perfect responsiveness and Lighthouse scores are part of the pitch.

## Product Purpose

Present Surga Indonesia Travel's private route planning so convincingly that a visitor either starts a consultation (form or WhatsApp) or a hiring client concludes "this person ships client-ready work." The route pages must feel like a working product: real pricing, departures, availability, day-by-day itineraries.

## Brand Personality

Editorial, grounded, operationally honest. "The route is the product" — copy talks about transfer timing and buffers, not paradise clichés. Luxury restraint rather than resort gloss: dark ink surfaces (#131b2e), warm sand accent (#ffdcc3), burnt caramel CTA (#8d4b00), flat panels, sharp corners, uppercase tracked labels, serif display headlines over full-bleed photography.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI slop: gradients-everywhere, rounded blobs, glassmorphism-as-default (owner's explicit non-negotiable: flat, sharp corners, ghost buttons).
- Mass-market OTA look (Booking.com/GetYourGuide density, badge spam, urgency banners).
- Template travel themes with centered-everything hero + three icon cards.

## Design Principles

1. **Photography carries the sell** — images must be eye-catching, sharp, and art-directed; UI stays flat and quiet around them.
2. **Operational honesty as luxury** — availability, dates, and pricing shown plainly; trust comes from specificity, not superlatives.
3. **Pixel-perfect at every width** — the site is itself the portfolio proof; a broken breakpoint is a lost client twice over.
4. **Editorial identity preserved** — serif display + tracked labels + flat panels is the committed voice; refine it, don't replace it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target: 4.5:1 body contrast, visible focus states, keyboard-navigable menus (already partly built: focus trap, Escape handling), reduced-motion alternatives for video and reveal effects. Lighthouse accessibility score matters for portfolio credibility.
